# Code of conduct

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions and commit messages in this repository, and anyone
representing the project elsewhere.

## Expected

- Assume the other person is competent. Ask what they were trying to achieve before
  concluding they were careless.
- Critique the code and the decision, not the person.
- Bring evidence. "Reproduced with this input, here is the output" beats an opinion.
- Respect the material. Contributors here work with other people's data; do not ask
  for captures, credentials or traffic the other person has no right to share.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change to keep the artifact focused.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information, including identifying traffic captures.
- Contributions whose main purpose is unauthorised interception, credential theft or
  circumventing authorisation.
- Sustained disruption after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

Report privately to [@nebula-eng](https://github.com/nebula-eng) or through the
repository's report form. Reports are handled confidentially. A maintainer may warn,
edit or remove a contribution, or block an account, depending on the behaviour and
its effect.

This follows the spirit of Contributor Covenant 2.1, narrowed to what one maintainer
can actually enforce.
